# Maa Soaps illustration assets

Self-contained, original code-native SVG illustrations made for the Maa Soaps sample storefront. No external image, font, script, dependency, certification, origin claim, or medical claim is embedded.

## Files

- `neem.svg`, `rose.svg`, `haldi.svg`, `oats.svg`: square 900 × 900 sample product illustrations. Use actual product photography and confirmed labels before launch.
- `plate-0.svg` through `plate-8.svg`: 1760 × 1240 open notebook spreads with a transparent outer canvas. Paper occupies approximately x=90–1670, y=270–970; the central fold is x=880. The art is intended as content supplied to the original ThreeUI page-curl engine through its existing scene hook.

## Visual language

Parchment `#f4efdf`; botanical ink `#4c5040`; sage `#a8b18a`; dusty rose `#cba19b`; turmeric `#d5b15f`; oat `#dbcfaf`. Fine ink outlines, botanical shapes, translucent washes, softly speckled soap, and restrained Georgia/Times serif typography. All nine spreads use the same fold, title baseline, caption baseline, and page geometry.

The illustrations represent concept products and ingredients. Ingredient composition, product details, and commercial claims must be supplied by the merchant.
